# Operational incident escalation — SBAR

**Situation**
- Service/component: [...]
- Current impact and affected scope: [...]
- Started / last known good: [...]
- Severity / urgency: [Only if grounded in the incident policy and evidence.]
- Immediate decision needed: [...]

**Background**
- Confirmed timeline and relevant changes: [...]
- Baseline vs. observed metrics (with timestamps/sources): [...]
- Logs, traces, alerts, dashboards, issue links: [...]
- Mitigations already **attempted**, with outcomes: [...]
- Permissions, operational constraints, rollback or recovery conditions: [...]

**Assessment**
- Supported interpretation: [...]
- Hypotheses not yet verified: [...]
- Alternative explanations / data gaps: [...]
- Risk of action vs. inaction: [...]

**Recommendation**
- Requested action or authorization: [...]
- Owner / decision maker: [Known / unassigned]
- Time sensitivity and why: [...]
- Validation / rollback / escalation criteria: [...]

**Status honesty:** [Observed degraded / mitigation in progress / verified recovered / unknown; never conflate proposed and completed.]
