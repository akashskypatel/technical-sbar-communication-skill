# Engineering handoff — SBAR

**Situation**
- Task or blocker: [...]
- Current state: [Unstarted / in progress / blocked / verified complete]
- Impact on the next work item: [...]

**Background**
- Repo, branch, revision, relevant files: [...]
- Requirements / constraints: [...]
- Changes made and decisions already recorded: [...]
- Evidence: diffs, logs, links, reproductions: [...]
- Build/test commands **actually executed** and their observed results: [...]
- Unexecuted checks and known failures: [...]

**Assessment**
- What is confirmed: [...]
- What is inferred and why: [...]
- Outstanding defects, uncertainties, risks: [...]
- Permission, dependencies, or environment blockers: [...]

**Recommendation**
- Desired next outcome (not a speculative prewritten patch): [...]
- Bounded next investigation or implementation scope: [...]
- Acceptance/verification criteria: [...]
- Owner/decision needed: [...]

**Do not imply that pending work, proposed fixes, or unrun checks are complete.**
